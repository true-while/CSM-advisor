# Eight Package Specifications

## Shared package contract

These are proposed package blueprints, not verified ESI catalog items. Initial state for every package is `design`; every course mapping is `pending`. No code, official course title, duration, PD flag, trainer, budget or delivery date is prefilled. The source plan's illustrative teaching-day estimates are intentionally not reusable defaults.

For each selected package, instantiate: package_id, package_version, owner role, route, target cohort/rings, evidence of need, outcome, entry gates, learning objectives, conceptual sequence, official mapping references, customer practice, assessment rubric, tangible outputs, exit/transfer condition, exclusions, feasibility gates and source/approval provenance.

For each cohort, carry the primary feature/app or persona/role learning-path selection from reference 02 into the package objectives, conceptual sequence and assessment. The lens is not a new package or route and does not change an existing package's entry criteria or outcome. A role workflow may need a bounded feature prerequisite; include it only when evidenced, and do not repeat or charge for the same official content twice. Preserve complete official course formats even when only one capability is the design focus.

Common controls apply to all eight: authorized active PD = Yes mapping before an MTT recommendation becomes operationally feasible; official duration and standard format retained; supported environment and prerequisites; current qualification, language and teaching zone; approved monthly budget and system total; individual-registration rules; customer-owned practice; approved resources only. Preparation, travel and exceptions remain separate dependencies. Package acceptance is a human decision, not a model status change.

## A1: First useful use

**Route and purpose:** Activate. Enable one cohort to complete a useful approved task safely and independently.

**Select when:** New access has not produced useful first use, learners lack a practical starting point, or a launch needs a bridge from announcement to application. Do not select merely because licenses were purchased.

**Entry gates:** Agreed task and success rubric, sponsor and practice owner, relevant license/feature access, supported environment, basic prerequisites and permitted data. A conditional access plan allows design, not hands-on execution.

**Ring sequence and learning:** Leaders select the task while technical leads validate readiness. Champions test an approved example. Advocates provide orientation and support boundaries. End users undertake eligible foundational instruction at the official duration and customer-owned application.

**Practice and outputs:** Repeat the task in a permitted setting; maintain a first-use checklist, safe examples, friction register and baseline/follow-up record. Use customer-created materials only after permission review.

**Assessment and transfer:** Demonstrate the task against the agreed rubric and repeat it without inappropriate support. Recommend Deepen when reliable first use exists but proficiency is the next gap. Pause if access fails.

**Illustrative horizon:** A two-week customer-led bridge, followed by a learning/practice cycle sized after mapping. The source's four-to-six-week cycle is an option, not a standard. No fixed trainer-day or credit estimate.

**Exclude:** Bespoke implementation, unresolved compliance decisions, unavailable features and promises of immediate new standard PD.

## A2: Pilot-to-launch readiness

**Route and purpose:** Activate. Produce evidence that a bounded pilot is operationally and instructionally ready for wider rollout.

**Select when:** Several roles are entering a new capability, readiness differs across groups, or the customer needs a controlled launch decision.

**Entry gates:** Bounded pilot and use case, sponsor and technical owner, documented environment support, individual-registration plan, practice ownership and a launch acceptance rubric. A small sponsor group is not presumed to meet PD registration minimums.

**Ring sequence and learning:** Run sponsor alignment and technical checks in parallel. Champions and technical leads receive the first eligible learning. Customer practice and remediation precede any broader wave. Advocates prepare the support pathway before end-user expansion.

**Practice and outputs:** Exercise the workflow, capture access/friction issues, prepare a customer-owned support map, evidence-backed launch checklist and revised audience plan.

**Assessment and transfer:** Sponsor and technical owner accept the readiness evidence, with critical access/governance issues resolved or the affected scope excluded. Select Deepen for remaining proficiency gaps or Extend only when value and receiving readiness justify it.

**Illustrative horizon:** Six to eight weeks with conditional learning waves, not two guaranteed teaching days. Preserve official formats.

**Exclude:** Production deployment, security certification and claims that attendance proves every learner is ready.

## D1: Role-based workflow proficiency

**Route and purpose:** Deepen. Improve repeatable execution, quality and judgment in one recurring role-specific workflow.

**Select when:** Appropriate recurring use exists but outputs are inconsistent, users rely on simple patterns, or workflow integration remains weak.

**Entry gates:** Basic use, a task rubric, supported workflow/environment, permitted practice tasks, manager support and protected practice time.

**Ring sequence and learning:** Champions bring safe realistic examples; technical leads validate dependencies; an eligible role-proficiency block is followed by application and feedback. A second advanced or troubleshooting learning block is included only if the gap and mapping justify it. Advocates coach practice; end users demonstrate task quality.

**Practice and outputs:** Compare task evidence with the baseline, build a reusable approved workflow pattern, describe common failure modes and prepare a customer-owned coaching guide.

**Assessment and transfer:** Show repeatable rubric improvement without a material quality, safety or process regression. Extend requires value evidence plus a receiving role with its own readiness. Technical defects go to support.

**Illustrative horizon:** Six to eight weeks with spaced practice. Do not assume two instructor days or turn conceptual practice weeks into course duration.

**Exclude:** Guaranteed productivity or financial savings and treating activity volume as proficiency.

## D2: Technical enablement and operational handoff

**Route and purpose:** Deepen. Equip a technical cohort to support an approved scenario and explain its operational boundaries.

**Select when:** Administrators, technical leads or builders need supported configuration, governance or integration understanding that is genuinely instructional.

**Entry gates:** Technical prerequisites, approved access, exact environment and lab compatibility, a bounded learning scope and an operational owner. Do not assume Commercial, GCC, GCC High or IL5 equivalence.

**Ring sequence and learning:** Technical leads are the central audience. Leaders validate business relevance; champions test the scenario. Use a customer-owned readiness review, eligible technical instruction at official duration, a controlled practice exercise and a handoff review. Downstream advocate/end-user guidance is scoped separately.

**Practice and outputs:** Produce a readiness checklist, permitted scenario demonstration, support ownership map and escalation boundaries. Keep implementation issues in a separately owned track.

**Assessment and transfer:** The technical owner demonstrates the scenario and safe limits. A new end-user cohort may enter Activate; another technical gap remains Deepen.

**Illustrative horizon:** Eight to twelve weeks or a different horizon required by official courses. No fixed teaching-day estimate and no unapproved non-consecutive split.

**Exclude:** Production changes, incident resolution, architecture sign-off and security or compliance approval.

## P1: Adoption recovery

**Route and purpose:** Protect. Restore useful adoption when an addressable skill or confidence gap is supported by evidence.

**Select when:** A comparable cohort deteriorates or dated feedback reveals repeatable friction, with a credible learning component. Urgent renewal alone is not sufficient.

**Entry gates:** Stable metric definition and appropriate comparison when quantitative deterioration is claimed, corroborating qualitative evidence, a blocker owner and agreement about what skilling can change. Without adequate data, offer diagnosis rather than a confirmed recovery prescription.

**Ring sequence and learning:** Leaders reset expectations; technical leads separate service/access issues; advocates gather approved aggregate themes. Champions test the corrected workflow. Eligible targeted instruction, customer-owned application and a recovery review follow.

**Practice and outputs:** Create a barrier register, owned remediation plan, revised safe workflow guide and cohort recovery evidence.

**Assessment and transfer:** Look for sustained appropriate use or task improvement with population and instrumentation changes disclosed. Return to Deepen for recurring proficiency work. Stop or redesign if no addressable learning gap is found.

**Illustrative horizon:** Four to eight weeks after diagnosis, adjusted to the actual intervention and evidence window. Reinforcement is conditional, not a second-day entitlement.

**Exclude:** Support-case closure, compliance adjudication, automatic renewal rescue and sentiment-only claims of restored business benefit.

## P2: Governance confidence and safe-use reset

**Route and purpose:** Protect. Reinforce understanding of already-authorized use, escalation and safe task boundaries.

**Select when:** Uncertainty is suppressing legitimate use and the customer's policy owner can state what is permitted.

**Entry gates:** Authoritative customer guidance, supported environment, reviewed scenarios and an owner for questions outside training. Unresolved policy decisions must be settled or excluded before affected practice.

**Ring sequence and learning:** Sponsor and technical owners clarify boundaries in parallel with champion scenario selection. Eligible learning reinforces approved concepts. Advocates facilitate permitted practice, and end users explain when to proceed, seek review or stop.

**Practice and outputs:** Prepare a customer-owned decision guide, safe examples, escalation path and an unresolved-question register. Do not distribute the underlying internal policy merely because a summary is permitted.

**Assessment and transfer:** Demonstrate correct scenario judgment and close or explicitly exclude critical ambiguities. Move to Activate or Deepen once safe use and access are ready.

**Illustrative horizon:** Four to six weeks, subject to current eligibility and policy readiness. Preparation needs separate treatment.

**Exclude:** Legal advice, regulatory certification, policy invention and guarantees that training establishes compliance.

## E1: Scale a proven workflow to a new role

**Route and purpose:** Extend. Transfer an evidenced workflow to a legitimate receiving role without assuming identical needs, environment or results.

**Select when:** A prior value review supports a safe reusable workflow and a new role has a real business need.

**Entry gates:** Completed sponsor-accepted value review, permitted artifact reuse, receiving sponsor and practice owner, a new baseline, environment validation and mapping review.

**Ring sequence and learning:** Leaders and champions explain original evidence and limits. Technical leads validate the receiving context. Prepare local advocates, deliver eligible role learning and follow it with customer practice and a replication review. Use Activate for a receiving cohort without basics.

**Practice and outputs:** Produce a context-adjusted workflow, receiving-cohort plan, expected-versus-observed comparison and an expansion decision.

**Assessment and transfer:** Require new-cohort evidence rather than extrapolating results. Return to Deepen if proficiency remains the barrier; broaden only when the receiving evidence and support capacity warrant it.

**Illustrative horizon:** Eight to twelve weeks or another justified cycle. Do not assume two validated waves.

**Exclude:** Automatic license/commercial recommendations, cross-account content sharing and counting existing champions as new unique reach.

## E2: Advocate network and sustained value campaign

**Route and purpose:** Extend. Establish a customer-owned network that sustains approved useful practice beyond formal instruction.

**Select when:** Several teams need local reinforcement, the original value case is credible and a sponsor can sustain advocate ownership.

**Entry gates:** Clear roles, approved reusable resources or licensed links, escalation ownership, a safe measurement method, receiving-cohort plan and local support capacity.

**Ring sequence and learning:** Champions and technical leads establish the safe pattern; advocates learn bounded coaching; end users join staggered ready cohorts. Include eligible learning waves only where mapped. Customer-owned practice exchanges are not automatically MTT delivery.

**Practice and outputs:** Create an advocate charter, draft communications calendar, reviewed resource directory, cohort handoff checklist and value decision log. All communications remain draft text.

**Assessment and transfer:** Demonstrate that the customer can sustain appropriate practice and credible outcome review without assumed continuous instructor support. Further expansion requires evidence and local capacity.

**Illustrative horizon:** Three to six months with fresh monthly feasibility reviews, not a reservation of future credits.

**Exclude:** Protected-content recordings or redistribution, automatic messages, unpaid ongoing trainer support, employee ranking and guaranteed financial returns.

## Release and retirement checklist

For a human reviewer to consider a version releasable, require design acceptance, active PD-eligible mapping, supported environment, qualified staffing feasibility, correct monthly budget and approvals, customer readiness, content permissions, assessment usability and a frozen plan version. The advisory skill reports the evidence and gaps; it does not release the package.

A retired, materially changed or incompatible mapping quarantines the affected package version. Preserve old customer-plan references; propose a remap and explicit reapproval rather than silently substituting new content. Change outcomes or official mapping in a major version, approved learning improvements in a minor version, and editorial corrections in a patch revision.
